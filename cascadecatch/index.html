Cascade Catch

Drop. Bounce. Multiply. Reach the target.

Cascade Catch is a physics-based puzzle game where every ball matters. Guide balls through pegs, multipliers, bouncers, and obstacles to score enough points to clear each level. Plan your drops carefully, use the board to your advantage, and discover creative ways to reach increasingly challenging targets.

How to Play

  • Drop balls from the top of the board
  • Use pegs, walls, and bouncers to alter their path
  • Hit multiplier zones to increase your score
  • Land in buckets that add, subtract, or multiply points
  • Reach the target score before you run out of balls

Physics-Based Strategy

  • Every bounce can change the outcome
  • Tap pegs to influence future ball paths
  • Use walls and bouncers to create scoring opportunities
  • Chain multipliers together for huge point totals
  • No two levels feel exactly the same

Progressive Challenge

  • Hundreds of unique board layouts
  • Increasing target scores and complexity
  • New obstacles and scoring combinations
  • Multiple ways to solve each puzzle
  • Reward strategic planning and experimentation

Features

  • Physics-driven ball movement
  • Multipliers, bonus buckets, and score modifiers
  • Peg tapping mechanics for additional control
  • Clean arcade-inspired presentation
  • Quick rounds that are easy to learn and difficult to master

Designed for Quick Play

  • Jump into a level in seconds
  • Perfect for short sessions or extended play
  • Satisfying chain reactions and scoring moments
  • Easy to understand, challenging to optimize
