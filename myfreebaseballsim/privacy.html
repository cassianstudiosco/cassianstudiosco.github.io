Privacy Policy

My Free Baseball Sim is published by Cassian Studios.

Gameplay and saves

The app does not require an account. League saves, rosters, results, and game settings are stored on your device. Cassian Studios does not receive those saves or settings through the app. You can remove the app’s local data by deleting the app.

Postgame ads and privacy choices

The app uses Google AdMob to show occasional full-screen ads after completed games. The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to present and manage ad privacy choices where applicable.

Google’s advertising and consent services may process device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad impressions and interactions, other app interactions, and crash, performance, or diagnostic information. Google can use this information to serve and measure ads, manage privacy choices, and maintain and improve its services. Ad delivery and personalization depend on your choices and the applicable settings.

When Ad Privacy Choices is available in the app, open it to review or change your ad privacy choices. For Google’s data practices, retention, and privacy controls, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Support and ad reports

If you email us for support or to report an ad, we receive the sender’s email address, the contents of the message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request or investigate the report. We keep support correspondence as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or where required by law.

You can ask about this policy or request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing cassianstudiosco@gmail.com. Use Help → Report an Ad in the app to report an inappropriate ad.
